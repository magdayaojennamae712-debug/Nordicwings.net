Cheapest Flights London to Madrid 2026 🇪🇸 – Airlines, Prices & Tips

📅 Updated July 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  ✈ LON → MAD

Madrid is one of Europe's great capital cities — world-class art, incredible food, electric nightlife, and some of the best football on the planet. From London, it's just a 2.5-hour direct flight. With Iberia, easyJet, and Ryanair all competing on the route, you can find fares from £35 one-way. Here's how to get the best deal.

✈ Airlines Flying London to Madrid

  • Iberia — Spain's flag carrier, flies from Heathrow (LHR) to Madrid Barajas (MAD). Excellent service, Avios miles, and often competitive pricing. From £45 one-way.
  • easyJet — Flies from Gatwick (LGW) and Luton (LTN). Cheapest fares from £35 one-way. Good for short city breaks.
  • British Airways — Heathrow to Madrid, multiple daily departures. Avios points, Club Europe upgrade option. From £50 one-way.
  • Vueling — Iberia's budget subsidiary, often the best value with checked luggage included. From £38 one-way.
  • Ryanair — Stansted (STN) to Madrid. Cheapest budget option from £29 one-way when booked early. Watch for add-on fees.
💡 Best value tip: Vueling often beats both Iberia and easyJet when checked luggage is factored in. Check Vueling.com directly — they sometimes offer exclusive deals with 23kg bag included that undercut comparison sites.

🗺️ Top Things to Do in Madrid

  • Museo del Prado — One of the world's finest art museums: Velázquez, Goya, El Greco. Book online to skip queues. Free entry 6–8pm daily.
  • Reina Sofía Museum — Modern art including Picasso's Guernica. A must-see.
  • Royal Palace of Madrid (Palacio Real) — Europe's largest royal palace by floor area, with lavish state rooms. Book timed entry.
  • Retiro Park — Madrid's beautiful central park with a boating lake, crystal palace, and rose garden. Free entry.
  • Mercado de San Miguel — Stunning iron-and-glass food market near Plaza Mayor. Perfect for tapas and vermouth.
  • Bernabéu Stadium — Real Madrid's legendary ground, with a world-class stadium tour. Book well ahead for match days.
🌡️ Weather tip: Madrid in summer (July–August) hits 35–40°C — brutally hot for sightseeing. May and October are the sweet spots: warm (20–24°C), comfortable for walking, and flights cost 40–60% less than peak summer.

🔑 Booking Tips

  • Book 6–8 weeks ahead for best fares; Easter and summer need 3+ months
  • Fly Tuesday or Wednesday — typically £20–£40 cheaper than weekends
  • Check Vueling direct — often cheapest with luggage included vs. budget carriers with add-on fees
  • Madrid Barajas (MAD) Terminal 4 is used by Iberia and BA — it's connected by free shuttle to Terminals 1–3
  • From airport to centre: Metro Line 8 runs to Nuevos Ministerios (12.50€, 25 minutes) — avoid official taxis unless you're splitting the €30 flat rate
  • Madrid's museums offer free entry on certain evenings — Prado is free 6–8pm, Reina Sofía 7–9pm weekdays

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest flight from London to Madrid?

Ryanair from Stansted from £29, easyJet from Gatwick from £35, and Vueling from £38. Book 6–8 weeks ahead and fly midweek.

How long is the flight from London to Madrid?

Approximately 2 hours 30 minutes direct. All major London airports have non-stop flights to Madrid Barajas (MAD).

Best time to visit Madrid?

May and October offer the best combination of pleasant weather (20–24°C), fewer crowds, and significantly cheaper flights than summer.

Cheapest month to fly London to Madrid?

January is cheapest from £35. February and November are also very affordable. Avoid July–August and Easter.

How do I get from Madrid airport to the city centre?

Metro Line 8 from Terminal 2/4 to Nuevos Ministerios costs €12.50 and takes 25 minutes. A taxi has a fixed €30 rate to the city centre.
