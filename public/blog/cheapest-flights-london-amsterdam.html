Cheapest Flights London to Amsterdam 2026 🇳🇱 – Airlines, Prices & Tips

📅 Updated July 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  ✈ LON → AMS

London to Amsterdam is one of the world's busiest flight routes — and at just 1 hour 20 minutes, it's also one of the fastest. easyJet, KLM, and British Airways compete fiercely on this route, keeping prices low. Fares start from £30 one-way, making Amsterdam one of the easiest European city breaks from London.

✈ Airlines Flying London to Amsterdam

  • easyJet — Most frequent service from Gatwick (LGW) and Luton (LTN). Cheapest fares from £30 one-way. Great for quick city breaks with hand luggage only.
  • KLM — Dutch flag carrier from Heathrow (LHR). Multiple daily flights, excellent service, SkyTeam miles. From £45 one-way.
  • British Airways — Heathrow to Schiphol, good for Avios points and Club Europe upgrades. From £50 one-way.
  • Transavia — Budget arm of KLM, sometimes cheaper on less popular times. Worth checking for off-peak travel. From £35 one-way.
🚆 Eurostar alternative: The direct Eurostar from London St Pancras to Amsterdam Centraal takes 3h52m and starts from £35. No airport check-in, no luggage fees, city-centre arrival. Compare both before booking — for many travellers Eurostar wins on convenience.

🗺️ Top Things to Do in Amsterdam

  • Anne Frank House — One of the most important historical sites in Europe. Book online weeks in advance — it sells out fast.
  • Rijksmuseum — Home to Rembrandt's Night Watch and Vermeer masterpieces. Allow 2–3 hours.
  • Van Gogh Museum — World's largest Van Gogh collection. Pre-book timed entry to skip queues.
  • Canal boat tour — The best way to see Amsterdam's UNESCO-listed canal ring. Evening tours are especially beautiful.
  • Vondelpark — Amsterdam's most famous park, perfect for picnics and people-watching.
  • Keukenhof Gardens (March–May) — 7 million tulips and flowers, 30 minutes from the city. A once-in-a-lifetime sight.
🚲 Getting around: Rent a bike — it's what locals do and it's the cheapest way to explore. MacBike and StarBikes rent from €12/day. Alternatively, the GVB tram network covers the whole city for €1.08 per ride with an OV-chipkaart.

🔑 Booking Tips

  • Book 4–6 weeks ahead — this route fills fast due to high demand
  • Compare flight vs Eurostar — when you factor in airport time, Eurostar can be faster door-to-door
  • Fly Tuesday or Wednesday — typically £15–£25 cheaper than weekend flights
  • From Schiphol, the direct train to Amsterdam Centraal runs every 10 minutes (15 min, €5)
  • Book museum tickets in advance — Anne Frank House sells out weeks ahead even off-season

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest flight from London to Amsterdam?

easyJet from Gatwick or Luton from £30. Book 4–6 weeks ahead and fly midweek for the best prices.

How long is the flight from London to Amsterdam?

Just 1 hour 20 minutes — one of the shortest international flights in Europe.

Flight or Eurostar — which is better?

Eurostar (3h52m, from £35) is city-centre to city-centre with no airport queues. Flying is faster in the air but add 2+ hours for airport procedures. Compare total journey time and price before deciding.

Cheapest month to fly London to Amsterdam?

January is cheapest from £30. Avoid April (tulip season) and July–August when prices peak.

How do I get from Schiphol airport to Amsterdam centre?

Direct train from Schiphol to Amsterdam Centraal runs every 10 minutes, takes 15 minutes, costs €5. It's the fastest and cheapest option — avoid taxis.
