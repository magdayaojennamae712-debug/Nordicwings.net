Cheapest Flights London to Paris 2026 🇫🇷 – Airlines, Prices & Tips

📅 Updated July 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  ✈ LON → PAR

London to Paris is the world's most popular international city-pair — and it's never been cheaper or easier to travel between them. You can fly one-way for as little as £28 with easyJet, or take the Eurostar from £29 for a city-centre-to-city-centre experience. Here's how to pick the best option and find the cheapest price.

✈ Airlines Flying London to Paris

  • easyJet — Flies from Gatwick (LGW) to Paris Orly (ORY) and CDG. Cheapest fares from £28 one-way. Great for hand-luggage trips.
  • British Airways — Heathrow (LHR) to Charles de Gaulle (CDG). Multiple daily flights, Avios points, flexible fares. From £55 one-way.
  • Air France — CDG to Heathrow and Gatwick. Good service, Flying Blue miles. From £50 one-way.
  • Vueling — Occasional flights via Barcelona. Good if combining trips. From £40 one-way.
🚆 Eurostar: often the smarter choice Eurostar runs from London St Pancras to Paris Gare du Nord in 2 hours 20 minutes from £29 one-way. No airport security queues, generous luggage allowance, and you arrive in the city centre. For most people, it beats flying on total journey time.

🗺️ Top Things to Do in Paris

  • Eiffel Tower — Paris's iconic landmark, 330m tall. Book timed entry online — queues without a ticket can be 3+ hours. Visit at dusk for the best light.
  • The Louvre — World's most visited museum: the Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, Winged Victory. Book tickets online, arrive at opening time to beat crowds.
  • Musée d'Orsay — Impressionist masterpieces (Monet, Renoir, Van Gogh) in a stunning converted railway station. Less crowded than the Louvre.
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral — Fully restored and reopened in December 2024 after the 2019 fire. More spectacular than ever.
  • Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur — Charming hilltop neighbourhood with panoramic city views and the white-domed basilica.
  • Seine river cruise — The classic way to see Paris's monuments from the water. Bateaux-Mouches from €17.
🌸 Best season tip: Spring (April–May) has the most beautiful light and the cherry blossoms — but it's busy and expensive. For the best value, visit in November or early March — fewer crowds, lower prices, and Paris in autumn/winter has a special atmosphere.

🔑 Booking Tips

  • Compare flight vs Eurostar — Eurostar often wins on total journey time and convenience
  • For flights, book 4–6 weeks ahead and fly Tuesday or Wednesday for lowest fares
  • Paris Orly (ORY) is closer to central Paris than CDG — check which airport your flight uses
  • From CDG, take the RER B train to central Paris (€12.10, 30 minutes)
  • Get a Paris Visite pass for unlimited metro/RER travel during your stay
  • Book Eiffel Tower and Louvre tickets weeks in advance — they sell out fast

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest way from London to Paris?

easyJet from £28 one-way by air, or Eurostar from £29 by train. Eurostar wins on convenience — no airport security, city-centre arrival, 2h20m journey.

How long is the flight from London to Paris?

About 1h15m in the air, but total journey time including airport check-in is 4–5 hours. Eurostar's 2h20m is often faster door-to-door.

Which Paris airport should I fly into?

Charles de Gaulle (CDG) is larger with better transport links (RER B, 30 min to centre). Orly (ORY) is closer to the south of Paris and smaller. Both are well connected.

Cheapest month to fly London to Paris?

January is cheapest from £28. November and February are also great value. Avoid July–August and Easter when prices double or triple.

Do UK citizens need a visa for Paris?

No visa required. UK passport holders can visit France for up to 90 days without a visa. Check current ETIAS requirements before travelling.
