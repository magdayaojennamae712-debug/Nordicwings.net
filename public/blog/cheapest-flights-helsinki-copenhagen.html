Cheapest Flights Helsinki to Copenhagen 2026 – Airlines & Tips

📅 Updated July 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  ✈ HEL → CPH

Copenhagen is one of Scandinavia's most beloved capitals — and from Helsinki, it's just a short hop of around 2 hours 20 minutes. Norwegian, Finnair and SAS all fly the route, with fares starting from as low as €57 one-way. Here's everything you need to know to get the cheapest ticket.

✈ Airlines Flying Helsinki to Copenhagen

Three airlines dominate the HEL–CPH route:

  • Norwegian — Budget carrier, cheapest fares from €57 one-way. Carry-on bag only on basic fares.
  • Finnair — Finland's national carrier, reliable and comfortable. Fares from €70. Frequent service with multiple daily flights.
  • SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) — Premium option with good connectivity if you're continuing to other Scandinavian cities. Fares from €85.

All three airlines operate non-stop (direct) flights from Helsinki-Vantaa (HEL) to Copenhagen Kastrup (CPH). Flight time is approximately 2 hours 20 minutes.

💡 Pro tip: Norwegian's cheapest fares often sell out fast. Book at least 6 weeks in advance and fly on a Tuesday or Wednesday for the lowest prices.

📅 Best Time to Fly Helsinki to Copenhagen

Prices vary significantly by season:

  • Cheapest: March, November, January — return fares from €120
  • Good value: May, September — pleasant weather, fares around €150 return
  • Most expensive: June, July, August — Copenhagen summer festival season, fares spike to €250–€350+
  • Christmas/New Year: Prices rise sharply — book 3+ months ahead

🗺️ What to Do in Copenhagen

Copenhagen is endlessly walkable and full of highlights:

  • Nyhavn — the iconic colourful waterfront, perfect for a coffee or a canal tour
  • Tivoli Gardens — historic amusement park in the city centre, open spring to autumn
  • The Little Mermaid — Copenhagen's most famous landmark, a short walk from the city centre
  • Christiansborg Palace — seat of the Danish Parliament, with free tower views over the city
  • Strøget — one of Europe's longest pedestrian shopping streets
  • Freetown Christiania — Copenhagen's famous alternative neighbourhood
🍽️ Food tip: Copenhagen is the world capital of New Nordic cuisine — try a traditional smørrebrød (open sandwich) or splurge on a tasting menu at one of the many Michelin-starred restaurants.

🔑 Booking Tips to Get the Lowest Price

  • Book 6–8 weeks in advance for the best fares — prices rise sharply closer to departure
  • Fly on Tuesday or Wednesday — cheapest days on this route
  • Check Norwegian's sale prices — they run regular flash sales on Scandinavian routes
  • Compare one-way vs return — sometimes two separate one-way tickets are cheaper
  • Copenhagen airport (CPH) is only 15 minutes by metro from the city centre
  • Consider Ryanair via other hubs if you have flexibility — can sometimes find fares under €50
