Cheapest Flights London to Rome 2026 🇮🇹 – Airlines, Prices & Tips

📅 Updated July 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  ✈ LON → ROM

Rome is one of the world's greatest cities — and from London, it's just a 2.5-hour flight away. Budget airlines like Ryanair and easyJet have made London–Rome one of Europe's most affordable long-weekend routes. You can fly one-way for as little as £25 if you book smart.

✈ Airlines Flying London to Rome

  • Ryanair — Flies from Stansted (STN) to Ciampino (CIA). Cheapest option from £25 one-way. Note: Ciampino is 40 minutes from central Rome by bus.
  • easyJet — Flies from Gatwick (LGW) and Luton (LTN) to Fiumicino (FCO). Good balance of price and convenience. From £35 one-way.
  • British Airways — Flies Heathrow (LHR) to Fiumicino (FCO). More legroom, Avios points, flexible fares. From £65 one-way.
  • ITA Airways — Italy's national carrier, Heathrow to Fiumicino. Good service, competitive on price. From £60 one-way.
  • Wizz Air — Flies Luton (LTN) to Fiumicino. Ultra-cheap when booked early, from £29. Strict on bag sizes.
💡 Airport tip: Ryanair's Ciampino (CIA) flights are cheap, but add €6–7 for the Terravision bus to Rome Termini. easyJet's Fiumicino (FCO) flights are slightly pricier but the Leonardo Express train (€14) gets you to Rome Termini in 30 minutes.

🗺️ Top Things to Do in Rome

  • The Colosseum — Rome's iconic amphitheatre, built in 70–80 AD. Book timed entry tickets online in advance — queues can be 2+ hours without a booking.
  • Vatican City — St Peter's Basilica, the Vatican Museums, and the Sistine Chapel. The most visited site in Rome — book months ahead in peak season.
  • Trevi Fountain — The world's most famous fountain. Visit at dawn to see it without crowds (and toss your coin).
  • Pantheon — 2,000-year-old temple, incredibly well preserved. Now requires a small entry fee (€5).
  • Roman Forum & Palatine Hill — Walk through the heart of Ancient Rome. Included with Colosseum ticket.
  • Trastevere — Rome's most charming neighbourhood, perfect for aperitivo and authentic trattorias.
🌡️ Best time tip: October is the sweet spot for Rome — temperatures are a perfect 18–22°C, tourist numbers drop sharply after summer, and fares are 40–60% cheaper than August. Spring (April–May) is also beautiful but busier and pricier due to Easter.

🔑 Booking Tips

  • Book 6–8 weeks ahead for best fares; for Easter and summer, book 3+ months ahead
  • Fly Tuesday or Wednesday — cheapest days, often £20–£40 cheaper than weekends
  • Compare FCO vs CIA — Ryanair's Ciampino is cheaper to fly but adds transfer costs
  • Book attraction tickets online in advance — Colosseum and Vatican sell out weeks ahead in summer
  • Rome's metro is simple and cheap (€1.50 per ride) — avoid taxis from the airport

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest flight from London to Rome?

Ryanair flies from Stansted to Ciampino from £25. easyJet from Gatwick to Fiumicino from £35. Book midweek, 6–8 weeks ahead.

How long is the flight from London to Rome?

Approximately 2 hours 30 minutes on direct flights. All major London airports have non-stop service to Rome.

FCO or CIA — which Rome airport?

Fiumicino (FCO) is more convenient — 30 minutes to city centre by Leonardo Express. Ciampino (CIA) is cheaper to fly into but requires a bus transfer.

Cheapest month to fly London to Rome?

January is cheapest, from £25 one-way. November and February are also great value. Avoid Easter, summer, and Christmas.

Do I need a visa to visit Rome from the UK?

Post-Brexit, UK passport holders can visit Italy for up to 90 days in any 180-day period without a visa. No ETIAS is currently required (check before travelling).
