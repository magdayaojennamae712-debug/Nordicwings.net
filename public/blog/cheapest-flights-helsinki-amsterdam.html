Cheapest Flights Helsinki to Amsterdam 2026 – Airlines & Tips

📅 Updated July 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  ✈ HEL → AMS

Amsterdam is one of Europe's most popular city break destinations — and from Helsinki, it's just a 3-hour direct flight. KLM and Finnair both fly non-stop to Amsterdam Schiphol, with fares from €85 one-way. Here's how to get the best deal.

✈ Airlines Flying Helsinki to Amsterdam

  • KLM Royal Dutch Airlines — Dutch national carrier with multiple direct daily flights. Fares from €85 one-way. Excellent service and a generous baggage policy. Partners with Air France and SkyTeam.
  • Finnair — Finland's flag carrier, direct flights from €90. Finnair has a long-standing partnership with KLM through the oneworld alliance. Good for earning Finnair Plus miles.
  • Transavia — Budget subsidiary of KLM, sometimes offers connecting flights via other hubs at lower prices. Worth checking if you have time flexibility.

All direct flights land at Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS), which is directly connected to Amsterdam Centraal station by train (15 minutes, €5).

💡 Pro tip: KLM often has the cheapest direct fares on this route. Check KLM.com directly as well as comparison sites — they sometimes offer exclusive web-only deals.

📅 Best Time to Fly Helsinki to Amsterdam

  • Cheapest: November, January, February — return fares from €150
  • Good value: September, October — beautiful autumn colours, fares around €170 return
  • Tulip season: April — very popular, prices rise. Book 3+ months ahead for Keukenhof visits
  • Most expensive: June–August and school holidays — demand is very high

🗺️ Top Things to Do in Amsterdam

  • Anne Frank House — moving and historically significant, book tickets well in advance online
  • Rijksmuseum — world-class art museum with Rembrandt and Vermeer masterpieces
  • Van Gogh Museum — the world's largest Van Gogh collection, must-book in advance
  • Canal boat tour — the best way to see Amsterdam's famous 17th-century canal ring
  • Jordaan district — charming neighbourhood with boutiques, galleries and cafés
  • Keukenhof Gardens (April–May) — the world's most beautiful flower gardens, 30 minutes from the city
🚲 Transport tip: Amsterdam is a cycling city. Rent a bike from Schiphol or the city centre — it's the cheapest and most authentic way to explore. The public transport (GVB) is excellent too.

🔑 Booking Tips

  • Book 6–8 weeks in advance for the best fares on direct flights
  • Fly on Tuesday or Wednesday — consistently the cheapest days
  • Check KLM.com directly — they often have exclusive lower prices vs. comparison sites
  • Add a checked bag at booking, not at the airport — much cheaper
  • From Schiphol, the direct train to Amsterdam Centraal takes 15 minutes (€5)
  • Consider an I Amsterdam City Card for unlimited public transport + museum entry
